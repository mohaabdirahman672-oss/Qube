Using Qube

How do I scan a math problem?

Open the Scan tab and choose Camera. Point it at a printed or handwritten problem and Qube solves it step by step. You can also upload a photo or type the problem.

The answer looks wrong. What should I do?

Qube is accurate on standard arithmetic, algebra, trigonometry and calculus, but it can misread handwriting or a blurry photo. Try a sharper, well-lit photo, or type the problem instead. Always check the steps before relying on an answer for graded work. If it keeps getting one wrong, email us the problem.

Why isn't my saved problem showing up?

Saved problems are stored with your account, so you need to be signed in. Guests can't save. Sign in, then open the Saved tab.

Free solves & Premium

How many free solves do I get?

20 AI solves every week, with or without an account. The count starts over each Monday. Flashcards and practice problems are always free.

What does Premium include?

Unlimited solves (fair use, up to 100 an hour) and AI worked examples on every flashcard. It's $9.99 a month or $79.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.

How do I cancel Premium?

Apple manages the subscription. On your iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Qube and tap Cancel. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for, then return to the free tier.

I paid, but Premium isn't showing.

Make sure you're signed in to the same Qube account, then go to Profile → Restore Purchases. If it still doesn't show, email us with the date of the purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your account

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password, or go to Profile → Settings → Change Password. We'll email you a reset link. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account and confirm. Your account and everything saved with it are deleted right away. If you signed in with Apple, you'll confirm with Apple once more. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Premium subscription, so cancel that in iPhone Settings first.
