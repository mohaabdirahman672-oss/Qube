What Qube is (and isn't)

Qube uses AI to generate step-by-step solutions to math problems you photograph or type. It's a study aid, not a certified tutor or textbook.

!

AI-generated answers can be wrong. Qube is right most of the time, but it can misread handwriting, misinterpret an ambiguous problem, or make a genuine reasoning error — like any AI system. Always check your work, especially before relying on an answer for graded work, an exam, or anything where being wrong actually matters.

Premium: unlimited, with fair use

Premium includes unlimited AI solves, subject to fair use: currently up to 100 solves per hour, far beyond normal study use. This ceiling exists only to stop automated or scripted use of the AI solver. If you reach it, you can solve again within the hour, and your weekly access is never affected.
