Legal · Data Practices

How Qube handles your data.

Qube solves math problems from a photo or typed text using AI. This page explains, in plain terms, exactly what we collect, why, and who we share it with — whether you're signed in or trying it as a guest.

01

Information we collect

Qube is built to work without an account — you can solve a limited number of problems as a guest before we ever ask you to sign in. What we collect depends on how you use the app.

Account information

If you create an account or sign in with Google or Apple, we (via our backend provider, Supabase) receive your email address and, if the provider shares it, your name and profile photo. Apple's "Hide My Email" relay is fully supported. We never see your Google or Apple password.

Math problems, photos, and typed text

When you scan or type a problem, the image or text is sent — through our own server, never directly from your device — to OpenAI's API to generate a step-by-step solution. We never store your problem photos. Problem text is not written to our database either, unless you choose to keep it: solutions you tap Save on, and flashcards you create, are stored with your account (see Retention).

Guest device data

If you use Qube without signing in, your device is assigned a random, anonymous identifier (not tied to your name, email, or Apple/Google ID). It's sent with each solve so our server can (a) count your free solves for the week and (b) apply a basic rate limit that prevents abuse of the free tier.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to Qube Premium, Apple processes the payment — we never see your card or billing details. We receive your subscription status (for example: active, in a free trial, or expired) so we can unlock Premium features.

Diagnostic and crash data

We use crash and error reporting tooling to catch bugs before they affect more people. This can include device model, OS version, app version, and the state of the app when a crash occurred — never your problem content.

02

How we use it

  • To generate step-by-step math solutions from your photo or typed problem
  • To create and secure your account, and keep you signed in
  • To enforce free-tier and rate limits fairly, and prevent abuse
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs
  • To respond when you contact support

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

03

Who we share it with

We share the minimum needed with a small set of infrastructure providers who help run Qube. None of them are permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

OpenAI

Receives your problem photo or text solely to generate a solution. Not used to train OpenAI's models under their API data usage policy.

Supabase

Our backend: hosts authentication, your account record, your saved problems and flashcards, weekly free-solve counts, and the server function that proxies solve requests.

Google Sign-In

Used only if you choose "Continue with Google." Governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Sign in with Apple

Used only if you choose "Continue with Apple." Governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

RevenueCat

Manages Premium subscription status: an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history with Apple. Used only if you subscribe or restore purchases.

Sentry

Receives crash and error reports so we can find and fix bugs. Never receives your problem content.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Qube, our users, or the public.

04

Retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. Problem photos are never retained — they exist only for the moment it takes to generate your solution. Problem text is not retained on our servers unless you choose to keep it: solutions you Save, and flashcards you create, are stored with your account so they sync across your devices, until you delete them or your account.

On your device only

Your last few solutions (the problem text and the solution — never the photo) are kept locally on your device so you can revisit them from the Home screen. They are never uploaded and are cleared when you sign out.

Free-solve counts

Our server keeps a weekly count of free solves, tied to your anonymous device identifier (or to your account, once you're signed in). It starts over each Monday. The identifier itself stays on your device and is removed if you delete the app.

05

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate account information
  • Delete your account and associated data

Delete your account any time in the app from Profile → Settings → Delete Account, or by emailing us directly (below). If you're in the EU/UK or California, you have specific rights under GDPR or the CCPA respectively — the same contact handles those requests.

06

Children's privacy

Qube is a study tool and may be used by students of many ages under a parent's or school's supervision, but the account itself is not knowingly directed at, or collecting data from, children under 13 without appropriate consent. If you believe a child has created an account without proper consent, contact us and we'll remove it.

07

Security

Data in transit between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). API keys for third-party AI services are kept server-side only and are never bundled into the app itself. No system is perfectly secure, but we design Qube so that a compromise of the app binary can't expose backend secrets.

08

International users

Qube is operated from the United States. If you use Qube from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S. and other countries where our providers operate, which may have different data protection laws than your home country.

09

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app. Continuing to use Qube after a change means you accept the updated policy.
